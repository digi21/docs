[Project limits](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-proyecto/limites-proyecto.md)

This tool calculates each project file's boundary. Boundaries are calculated quickly from the file's minimum and maximum coordinates. However, the dialog can also calculate a precise boundary at a specified resolution, representing files with irregular distributions more accurately than the rectangle defined by their minimum and maximum coordinates.
