# Delete triangles by elevation difference
<!-- id: borrar-triangulos-por-diferencia-de-cota -->
<!-- origen: 2cc8c81d7a03c4a318e6649769620c337977f90f -->

[Delete TIN triangles](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-edicion-tin/borrar-triangulos-del-tin.md)

This tool displays information about the elevation differences along triangle sides and deletes triangles with a side exceeding a specified difference. In the dialog, enter a value in meters in **Maximum height difference** and click **Delete** to remove triangles with any side exceeding this value.
