# Side lengths
<!-- id: tamano-de-los-lados -->
<!-- origen: d80b00661682412a7070ab2769cf4107dfa352ad -->

[Delete TIN triangles](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-edicion-tin/borrar-triangulos-del-tin.md)

This tool displays triangle side length information and deletes triangles with sides longer than a specified limit. Its dialog shows ten side length ranges, with the count and percentage in each range.

![Triangles size dialog](../../images/image-82.png)

Enter a value in meters in **Maximum distance** and click **OK** to delete triangles with any side longer than this value. The information updates for the remaining triangles, allowing you to delete again.

This tool can also be run from the [command line](../desde-linea-de-comando/linea-de-comando-borrar-triangulos-por-longitud-de-lado.md).
