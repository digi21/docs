[Compute](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-imagen/imagen-calcular.md)

This tool calculates images derived from the current one by reducing resolution. The dialog contains the following fields:

* **Pyramidal level**: numeric value specifying the resolution reduction factor. For example, 2 halves the resolution.
* **Interpolation**: choose one of three interpolation methods: Nearest neighbor, Bilinear or Bicubic.
