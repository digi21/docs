# Application panes
<!-- id: paneles-de-la-aplicacion -->
<!-- origen: dff10c0f3a230f2e67f5dcec1562910ad037507d -->

[User interface](../interfaz-de-usuario.md)

Application panes are additional windows displaying information or tools useful to keep visible while working with a document. Turn them off using their checkboxes on the [Home tools](../../fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-inicio/) ribbon. Move a pane anywhere by dragging its title bar with the left mouse button and releasing it at the desired location. Click the pin icon in its upper right corner to hide it automatically when unused.

The available panes are:

* [**Properties**](/digi3d-ai/referencia/paneles/propiedades.md): shows or hides the current document's properties.
* [**Output**](/digi3d-ai/referencia/paneles/resultados.md): shows or hides the window where tools report their activity.
* [**Project**](panel-proyecto.md): shows or hides the project window listing its files.
* [**LiDAR**](panel-lidar.md): shows or hides quick classification tools for LiDAR files.
* [**Background images**](panel-imagenes-de-fondo.md): shows or hides the background image window.

Use the options on the [Home Window](../../fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-inicio/ventana.md) ribbon to show or hide these panes.
