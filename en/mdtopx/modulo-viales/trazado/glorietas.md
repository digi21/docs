# Roundabouts

When a roundabout is entered as a new alignment, the program displays a dialog with the selected calculation method enabled and results represented by its center and radius.

Create a roundabout with one of the options on the [Horizontal tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-trazado.md).

![Roundabout dialog](../../../images/image-107.png)

Displayed data:

* **Calculation option**: method for calculating the new roundabout:
  * _Three points_: three points on the roundabout alignment.
  * _Center and point_: center and a point on the alignment.
  * _Center and radius_: center and radius.
  * _Two points and radius_: two alignment points and radius.
  * _Point 1, Point 2, Point 3_: calculation inputs, enabled or disabled according to the selected method.
* **Results**: calculated data, acting as inputs or outputs depending on the method:
  * _Radius_: roundabout radius in meters.
  * _Center coordinates_: roundabout center. _Two points and radius_ offers two possible solutions; choose the desired one.
  * _Total length_: alignment length in meters.
  * _Inner area_: area enclosed by the alignment, in square meters.

Accepting the calculated data opens a [dialog](insertar-elemento-curvo.md) showing the new roundabout alignment's vertices and tangent points.
