# Insert interior vertex
<!-- id: insertar-vertice-interior -->
<!-- origen: 3b7f1b6b7cb8059ed3cb70b6aea81d3530adddef -->

[Compute new vertical alignment dialog](calcular-alzado-nuevo/)

This tool inserts a new vertical alignment vertex without changing its current geometric conditions. Enter the desired point's chainage and either its elevation directly or its slope from the previous point.

The program determines whether current geometry permits insertion. If not, the point cannot be inserted. It displays the preceding and following points for the entered chainage as position references.
