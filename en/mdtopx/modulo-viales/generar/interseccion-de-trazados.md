# Alignment intersections
<!-- id: interseccion-de-trazados -->
<!-- origen: 0253169362bb53db71251a040ff9a6c135021a50 -->

[Roads module](/mdtopx/modulo-viales/)

To calculate intersections between alignments, the program displays a dialog for selecting horizontal alignments and their vertical alignments, finding intersections and elevation differences.

The upper-left list contains horizontal alignments and the lower-left list their vertical alignments. The right list contains selected elements. Use **Add** to populate it or **Delete** to remove items.
