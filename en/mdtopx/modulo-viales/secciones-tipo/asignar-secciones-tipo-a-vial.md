# Assign templates to a road
<!-- id: asignar-secciones-tipo-a-vial -->
<!-- origen: e46150662f139f75c2e1d6486a58a24b78019856 -->

[Template cross sections](/mdtopx/modulo-viales/secciones-tipo/)

This tool assigns one or more template cross sections along a road. A fully defined road needs at least one template starting at the alignment's beginning, usually chainage 0. If the first template starts later, the initial road portion is ignored because it has no template definition.

An added template applies from its specified chainage to the end or until another template is encountered.

The program displays the road being edited and its total length to help assign templates.

It also lists currently stored templates defined in [Template list](listado-secciones-tipo.md).

![Assign templates to a road dialog](../../../images/image-93.png)

To **Add** a template, select it from the list and specify its starting chainage; it appears in the list on the left. You can also **Delete** a template from the road definition.
