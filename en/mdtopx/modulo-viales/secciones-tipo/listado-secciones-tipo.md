[Template cross sections](/mdtopx/modulo-viales/secciones-tipo/)

This tool manages template cross sections for later application to alignments to complete road definitions. Its dialog lists available templates and previews the selected one.

![List of templates dialog](../../../images/image-91.png)

Buttons **Add**, **Edit**, **Duplicate** or **Erase** manage templates.

Adding or editing opens a [dialog](seccion-tipo.md) with the template's characteristics.
