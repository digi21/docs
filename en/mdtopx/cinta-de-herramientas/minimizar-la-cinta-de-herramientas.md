# Minimize the ribbon
<!-- id: minimizar-la-cinta-de-herramientas -->
<!-- origen: 5cbcb18c831dd0c9819463a6279d84c32b3bd55c -->

[Ribbon](/mdtopx/cinta-de-herramientas/)

The ribbon helps you find commands needed for a task. Commands are organized into logical groups on tabs, each associated with an activity such as DTM tools or Edit. Some tabs appear only when needed to reduce screen clutter.

You cannot remove the ribbon or replace it with earlier MDTop versions' toolbars and menus. You can minimize it to enlarge the workspace.

![Full ribbon](../../images/cinta-de-herramientas.jpg)

![Minimized ribbon](../../images/cinta-de-herramientas-minimizada.jpg)

To keep the ribbon minimized:

* Click [Customize Quick Access Toolbar](personalizar-la-barra-de-herramientas-de-acceso-rapido.md).
* Click Minimize the Ribbon in the list.
* To use the minimized ribbon, click a tab and then the desired option or command.\
  For example, click Home and select a command. The ribbon then minimizes again.

To restore the ribbon:

* Click [Customize Quick Access Toolbar](personalizar-la-barra-de-herramientas-de-acceso-rapido.md).
* Click Minimize the Ribbon in the list.

For quick minimization, double-click the active tab name. Double-click a tab again to restore the ribbon. CTRL+F1 also minimizes or restores it.

Keyboard shortcuts remain available while the ribbon is minimized. See [Use the ribbon with the keyboard](/mdtopx/cinta-de-herramientas/utilizar-la-cinta-de-herramientas-mediante-teclado.md) for more information.
