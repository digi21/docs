# Cell information
<!-- id: informacion-de-celda -->
<!-- origen: 74e9622731e60f1971fbde9d7e88e63d70faaceb -->

[Elements](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-editar/editar-elementos.md)

This dialog displays and edits cell properties. Cells divide a grid digital model and are defined by a rectangle's four corner points, or a square if height and width match. Coordinates are displayed, but only elevations can change because grid cell positions are fixed. For a previously smoothed grid, X and Y derivatives and the XY mixed derivative are also shown.
