[Elements](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-editar/editar-elementos.md)

Requesting information about a point in a linear entity or grid digital model displays these properties:

* XYZ position.
* For a previously smoothed grid, X and Y derivatives and the XY mixed derivative.
