# Parallel
<!-- id: paralela-2 -->
<!-- origen: 7d024af5f6e4874b6d16957f5460d6aeeb477c64 -->

[Edit elements](../../fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-editar/editar-elementos.md)

This tool generates parallels to a linear entity. Its dialog offers two calculation methods:

* **User-defined values**: enter XY and Z displacements separately. For a horizontal offset, also choose left or right of the entity.
* **Through point**: graphically specify a point through which the new parallel entity should pass.

After setting parameters, left-click the linear entity when prompted.
