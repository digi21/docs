This tool displays the document's information distribution by entity elevation and can delete information by elevation.

Fields:

* **Histogram**: chart with elevation on Y and element count on X.
* **Delete in boundaries**: optionally deletes elements within specified minimum and maximum elevation limits.
* **Only in the selection**: acts only on currently selected points.
