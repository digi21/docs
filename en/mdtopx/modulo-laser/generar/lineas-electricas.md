# Power lines
<!-- id: lineas-electricas -->
<!-- origen: 17cd67ef641601083a4b7b7604d9ed1944af37ec -->

[Generate from LiDAR](../../fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-archivos-lidar/calcular-a-partir-de-lidar.md)

Finds and classifies power lines and their supporting towers.

![Power lines dialog](../../../images/image-159.png)

Required parameters:

* **Max distance among points**: maximum neighbor distance, based on point density.
* **Height tolerance**: point elevation tolerance for locating wires.
* **Type for wires**: classification for points on wires.
* **Type for towers**: classification for points on supporting towers.
