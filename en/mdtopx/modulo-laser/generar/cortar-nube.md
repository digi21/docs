# Cut cloud
<!-- id: cortar-nube -->
<!-- origen: 26c515f47757aaa63b9460b1dfe1a2d9ec479da4 -->

[Generate from LiDAR](../../fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-archivos-lidar/calcular-a-partir-de-lidar.md)

Cuts a point cloud along a user-defined line. Intended for tunnel or gallery scans, selecting points from a particular part of the tunnel.

![Cut cloud dialog](../../../images/image-154.png)

Required parameters:

* **Line file**: previously recorded line, such as the scanner station sequence from [Get stations](/mdtopx/modulo-laser/generar/obtener-estaciones.md).
* **Cut type (what to delete)**: part removed by a _Horizontal_ or _Vertical_ cut.
