# Material properties
<!-- id: propiedades-de-material -->
<!-- origen: fa411e181aa49f6b7844f25bc83e19ba3cd9845a -->

[Home tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-inicio/)

This dialog edits the lighting received by a digital model. It is only available with an active digital model.

![Material properties dialog](../../images/image-75.png)

The displayed parameters range from zero to one hundred:

* **Speculate**: color of reflections that an object can produce. It depends on the angle of incidence of the light.
* **Brightness**: amount of reflections or highlights on an object, depending on whether it is rough or polished.
* **Emission**: color of the entire object, whether illuminated or not.

The dialog remains open until you explicitly close it, allowing you to continue manipulating the document.
