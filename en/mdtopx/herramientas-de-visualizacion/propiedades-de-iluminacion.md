# Lighting properties

[Home tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-inicio/)

This dialog edits the lighting received by a digital model. You can change light values and the light source coordinates. It is only available with an active digital model.

![Lighting Properties dialog](../../images/image-74.png)

The displayed parameters range from zero to one hundred:

* **Lighting**:
  * _Ambient_: ambient light intensity. This is the ambient color, corresponding to the unlit part of a material. It is often the same as the diffuse color.
  * _Diffuse_: diffuse light intensity. The diffuse color is the color emitted by an illuminated material.
  * _Speculate_: specular light intensity.
* **Coordinates of the light**: moves the light source by setting its X, Y and Z coordinates.

The dialog remains open until you explicitly close it, allowing you to continue manipulating the document.
