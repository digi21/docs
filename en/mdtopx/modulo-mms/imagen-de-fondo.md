[MMS tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-mms.md)

Tools for background 360-degree images in the MMS module:

* **Prev image**
* **Next image**

Loads the next or previous background image when a project with 360-degree images is loaded.

Images appear in the [Background images pane](../introduccion/paneles-de-la-aplicacion/panel-imagenes-de-fondo.md).
