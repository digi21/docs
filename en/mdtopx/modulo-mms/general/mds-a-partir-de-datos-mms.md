# DSM from MMS data
<!-- id: mds-a-partir-de-datos-mms -->
<!-- origen: a952f57edb9261d9aef8711f88433f532231b159 -->

[MMS General tab](./)

Calculates a 3D digital surface model from a Mobile Mapping System cloud: a DTM of ground, asphalt and white line points, plus separate three-dimensional models of recorded objects based on classified points.

Required data:

* **Ground-defining types**: ground, asphalt, road marking and other ground point classifications.
* **Object-defining types**: classifications of road objects such as signs, streetlights and guardrails.
* **Maximum distance among points**: maximum spatial neighbor distance.
