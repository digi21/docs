Tools for road point clouds in the [MMS module](../):

* [**Bare ground**](clasificar-suelo-de-trazado.md): classifies ground and road element points, the first step before finding road elements.
* [**Asphalt**](clasificar-lineas-de-vial.md): finds road elements after ground detection.
* [**Road lines**](buscar-lineas-de-vial/): generates asphalt boundaries, road markings and cross sections.
* [**Road inventory**](buscar-objetos-en-el-trazado.md): finds objects on or near the road.
* [**MMS database**](base-de-datos-mms.md)
* [**Visibility**](visibilidad.md)
* [**Buffer**](buffer-en-datos-mms.md): removes points far from the area of interest.
* [**Guardrail**](guardarrail.md)
