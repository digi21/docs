# Delete in limits
<!-- id: borra-en-limites -->
<!-- origen: 385b0105e198b0a128330ba10d5ca40ca6833171 -->

[VirtuaLand Edit](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-virtualand/virtualand-editar.md)

This tool deletes grid points inside or outside boundaries stored as closed lines in drawing files \(DIGI, AutoCAD or MicroStation format). Use it to delete information where you do not want calculations such as contours or profile projection.

![Delete in limits dialog](../../images/image-116.png)

Complete the following fields:

* **Selection of the boundaries**: loads drawing entities from a file as boundaries. These entities must be closed.
* **Where erase**: specifies where to delete triangles, with two options:
  * _Inside limits_
  * _Outside limits_
