# Compute from LiDAR
<!-- id: calcular-a-partir-de-lidar -->
<!-- origen: 1b5e3d17b82574870d902cc318ad33c89a4904aa -->

[Laser tab](./)

* [**Model**](../../modulo-laser/obtener-modelo-digital/): generates a 2D or 3D model.
* [**Contours**](../../modulo-laser/generar/curvado-a-partir-de-lidar.md): generates contours directly from the point cloud.
* [**Profiles**](../../herramientas-mdt/perfiles/): generates longitudinal and cross profiles from LiDAR data.
* [**Grid**](../../modulo-laser/generar/rejilla-a-partir-de-lidar.md): generates a regular grid DEM.
* [**Project**](../../modulo-laser/generar/proyeccion-sobre-la-nube-de-puntos.md): assigns elevations to drawing file entities.
* [**DSMn**](../../modulo-laser/generar/modelo-digital-de-superficies-normalizado.md): generates a grid of heights above ground.
* [**Get stations**](../../modulo-laser/generar/obtener-estaciones.md): obtains terrestrial scanner station positions.
* [**Cut point cloud**](../../modulo-laser/generar/cortar-nube.md): extracts a section of the cloud along a user-defined alignment.
* [**Split in elements**](../../modulo-laser/generar/divide-en-elementos.md): classifies terrestrial points by surface.
* [**Generate raster**](../../modulo-laser/generar/generar-imagen.md): generates a raster image showing elevation, classification, intensity or other properties.
* [**Classify surfaces**](../../modulo-laser/generar/clasificar-superficies.md): classifies and assigns heights to closed drawing entities projected onto LiDAR data.
* [**Cloud compare**](../../modulo-laser/generar/comparar-nubes.md): compares elevations and classifications in two clouds.
* [**Power lines**](../../modulo-laser/generar/lineas-electricas.md): finds and classifies power lines and towers.
* [**Comparison 3D**](../../modulo-laser/generar/comparar-nubes-3d.md): compares terrestrial object point clouds in full 3D.
