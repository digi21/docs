[VirtuaLand tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-virtualand/)

This group contains the main VirtuaLand module tools:

* [**Extract breaklines**](../../modulo-virtualand/extraer-lineas-de-ruptura.md): automatically extracts breaklines defining the point set.
* [**Compare DEM**](../../modulo-virtualand/comparar-dos-dem.md): compares two models covering the same area.
* [**Generate DEM derived**](../../modulo-virtualand/generar-dem-derivado.md): generates a model from the current one by cropping it or reducing its point density.
