# VirtuaLand Edit
<!-- id: virtualand-editar -->

[VirtuaLand tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-virtualand/)

This group contains VirtuaLand module editing tools:

* [**Smoothing**](../../modulo-virtualand/suavizado.md): smooths the DEM using cubic splines.
* [**Fill gaps**](../../modulo-virtualand/rellenar-huecos.md): fills interior gaps in the grid by interpolating adjacent points.
* **Delete vertex by fence**: defines a fence by entering points with the left mouse button and finishing with the right button. Points inside are selected. Left-click to confirm deletion or right-click to cancel.
* [**Delete in limits**](../../modulo-virtualand/borra-en-limites.md): deletes grid points inside or outside a boundary.
* [**Delete by height**](../../modulo-virtualand/borra-por-altitudes.md): deletes points according to their elevation.
