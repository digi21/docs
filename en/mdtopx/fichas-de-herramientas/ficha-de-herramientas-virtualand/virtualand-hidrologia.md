The [hydrology](../../modulo-virtualand/hidrologia/) tools of a DEM are grouped in this section:

* [**Basins**](../../modulo-virtualand/hidrologia/cuencas.md): the watershed of each outlet, with its parameters, the time of concentration and the peak discharge of the rational method of the Spanish standard 5.2-IC
* [**Hydrology grids**](../../modulo-virtualand/hidrologia/rejillas-hidrologicas.md): accumulation, slope, TWI, SPI, LS factor and height and distance to the stream, each one in a new DEM
* [**Flood zones**](../../modulo-virtualand/hidrologia/zonas-inundables.md): what is flooded with a water height in the streams, fixed or from a discharge
* [**Ponding**](../../modulo-virtualand/hidrologia/encharcamiento.md): where the water of a rainfall stays in the depressions of the terrain
