# DTM view
<!-- id: vista-de-mdt -->
<!-- origen: c7eb18e417bbb39ef055d08e35669935d0c33124 -->

[DTM tools tab](./)

This group contains DTM view management commands:

* [**Surface**](../../herramientas-de-visualizacion/vista-de-superficie-de-mdt.md): selects how to display the DTM. Requires a digital model document or digital models loaded as references.
* [**DTM Objects**](../../herramientas-de-visualizacion/encender-apagar-objetos-del-mdt.md): shows or hides objects loaded over the digital model. Load them with [Add objects](../../herramientas-de-edicion-de-la-triangulacion/poner-cubiertas.md) in [TIN edition DTM Objects](../ficha-de-herramientas-edicion-tin/objetos-mdt.md).
* [**Surface Types**](../../herramientas-de-visualizacion/listado-de-tipos-de-superficie.md): shows or hides model triangles assigned a surface type.
* **Base**: displays a surface at the model's minimum elevation with the same plan boundary, joined vertically to the outer model cells or triangles to strengthen the three-dimensional appearance.
* **Breaklines**: shows or hides retained model breaklines. Requires a digital model document or digital models loaded as references.
* **Contours**: displays quick contours for elevation analysis. These consist only of line segments formed by intersections between triangles and elevation planes. If active, contour lines are hidden; if inactive, a dialog configures their display. Requires a digital model document or digital models loaded as references.
* **Smoothing points**: for a [smoothed](../../modulo-virtualand/suavizado.md) grid digital model, selects how many points display each cell, representing its surface more accurately.
* **Light**: enables or disables the light source that gives the model view relief. Sometimes you do not want different model element colors depending on lighting. Change lighting with [Light](../../herramientas-de-visualizacion/propiedades-de-iluminacion.md) on the [Home tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-inicio/).
