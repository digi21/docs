# Civil products
<!-- id: productos-civil -->
<!-- origen: 547557bf564e5542bcad55fd0030aad2cc2cbb75 -->

[DTM tools tab](./)

This group contains commands generating civil engineering documents:

* [**Contours**](../../como/como-curvado.md): generates contour lines.
* [**Profiles**](../../como/como-perfiles.md): generates longitudinal and cross profiles.
* [**Volumes**](../../como/como-cubicacion.md): calculates volumes between two digital models.
* [**Slope vectors**](../../herramientas-mdt/vectores-de-pendiente.md): calculates vectors defining a DTM's slope.
* [**Project surfaces**](../../herramientas-mdt/proyectar-superficies.md): projects surfaces onto a digital model, either raising a surface above the DTM according to its centroid's height difference or generating a centroid with its relative height above the DTM.
* [**Platforms**](../../como/como-plataformas.md): calculates platform projections onto a DTM.
* [**Project**](../../como/como-proyeccion-sobre-mdt.md): projects mapping onto the digital model.
* [**Check**](../../como/como-comprobar-mdt.md): checks digital model elevations.
