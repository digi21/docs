[Ribbon](/mdtopx/cinta-de-herramientas/)

![LiDAR classification ribbon](../../images/ficha-de-herramientas-clasificar-lidar.jpg)

This tab contains commands for classifying LiDAR points measured by airborne laser devices, available with the [Laser module](../modulo-laser/).

The commands are grouped as follows:

* [**Find points**](../modulo-laser/buscar-puntos/)
* [**Geometric shapes**](../modulo-laser/formas-geometricas/)
