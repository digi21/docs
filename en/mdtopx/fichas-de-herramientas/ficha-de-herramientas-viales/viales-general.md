# Roads General

[Roads tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-viales/)

This group contains general Roads module tools:

* [**Properties**](../../modulo-viales/general/informacion-del-documento-viales.md): displays information about the current roads document and its elements, and configures the longitudinal profiles shown in vertical alignment view.
* **Horizontal - Vertical**: switches between plan view of designed roads and their longitudinal profiles. F3 also switches views. Tools requiring entity selection in either view switch automatically when needed.
* [**Compute curves**](../../modulo-viales/general/calcula-curvas.md): calculates curved elements from some of their defining parameters.
* **Reverse direction**: reverses the vertex order of a horizontal alignment and its associated vertical alignments. Select the alignment with the left mouse button. After confirmation, the corresponding vertical alignment view updates automatically.
* [**Label road**](../../modulo-viales/general/rotular-vial.md): displays elements helping you interpret alignments. These are marks perpendicular to the road direction, optionally accompanied by text describing road properties at that point.
