# Superelevation list
<!-- id: tabla-de-peraltes -->
<!-- origen: 168db239a42de1e00b7e999a71fe9deeeac13538 -->

[Roads tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-viales/)

This group manages superelevation tables applied to a road:

* [**Superelevation list**](/mdtopx/modulo-viales/tabla-de-peraltes/lista-de-tablas-de-peraltes.md): opens management of the program's stored superelevation tables.
* [**Attach**](../../modulo-viales/tabla-de-peraltes/asignar-peraltes-a-vial.md): calculates a road's superelevation according to its curves. The program switches to horizontal alignment view and asks you to select the alignment, then opens a calculation dialog.
