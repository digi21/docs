# Templates
<!-- id: secciones-tipo -->
<!-- origen: 2d5ae4abf5caf6266d52127cc73dc860c3d737c7 -->

[Roads tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-viales/)

This group manages templates applied to a road:

* [**Templates**](../../modulo-viales/secciones-tipo/listado-secciones-tipo.md): opens management of the templates stored by the program.
* [**Attach**](../../modulo-viales/secciones-tipo/asignar-secciones-tipo-a-vial.md): associates templates with a road. The program switches to horizontal alignment view and asks you to select the alignment whose templates you want to apply or modify, then opens a dialog to establish these associations.
