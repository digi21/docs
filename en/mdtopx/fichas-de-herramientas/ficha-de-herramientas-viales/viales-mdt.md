# Roads DTM
<!-- id: viales-mdt -->
<!-- origen: e5b6e6bd0fe7e02ec8d415a4bb3f642c97af5e06 -->

[Roads tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-viales/)

This group manages the DTM used to obtain alignment elevations:

* **Load**: loads a DTM for calculations requiring terrain elevations, such as longitudinal terrain profiles, cross profiles or terrain modified by newly designed roads. Any previously loaded DTM is unloaded automatically.
* **Unload**: unloads the current DTM. Available only when one is loaded.
