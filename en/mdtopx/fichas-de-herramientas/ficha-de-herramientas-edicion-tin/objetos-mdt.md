DTM objects are additional models representing objects above the ground surface, such as buildings, bridges or trees. They are excluded from operations such as contours or profiles, but included when calculating a true orthophoto, for example. Show or hide them with [DTM Objects](/mdtopx/herramientas-de-visualizacion/encender-apagar-objetos-del-mdt.md).

This group contains the following tools for managing these objects:

* [**Add objects**](../../herramientas-de-edicion-de-la-triangulacion/poner-cubiertas.md): inserts objects into the digital model.
* [**Information**](../../herramientas-de-edicion-de-la-triangulacion/informacion-de-objeto.md): displays a dialog for viewing and editing the properties of an object included in the DTM.
* **Delete**: deletes objects inserted into the current document. Select them graphically with the left mouse button.
