# TIN view

[TIN edition tab](/mdtopx/fichas-de-herramientas/ficha-de-herramientas-edicion-tin/)

The View group contains the following tools:

* [**TIN Properties**](../../introduccion/paneles-de-la-aplicacion/panel-propiedades/panel-propiedades-documento-tin.md): displays additional characteristics of the current document.
* **View with selection**: displays only the selection so you can work on an area without the rest of the digital model getting in the way, such as a pipe without the ground behind it. First select the points or triangles to isolate with any [selection](../ficha-de-herramientas-editar/editar-elementos.md) tool, then click the button. Only triangles whose three points are selected are displayed. Within this view, selections only choose visible elements; select again and click the button to isolate a smaller part. **Fit window** frames only visible elements, and [Fill one 3D gap](../../herramientas-de-edicion-de-la-triangulacion/rellenar-huecos-3d.md#with-a-view-with-selection) works only with visible triangles. Reference digital models are displayed in full. To display the entire digital model again, click the button with nothing selected and confirm. If a point's elevation or position changes in this view, that point and its triangles are no longer displayed.
