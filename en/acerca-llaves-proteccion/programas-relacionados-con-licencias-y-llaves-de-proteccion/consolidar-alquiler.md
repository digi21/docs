# Consolidate rental
<!-- id: consolidar-alquiler -->
<!-- origen: 61837a0a2ccfaf72d9260339bdbf8b905dd82827 -->

This program downloads newly purchased rental licenses from our servers and stores them in your dongle.\
\
If the computer hosting the dongle has no internet access, use [Consolidate rental offline](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/programas-relacionados-con-licencias-y-llaves-de-proteccion/consolidar-alquiler-offline/).\
\
To add rented licenses:

1. Click **Start/Programs/Digi21.NET/Licenses/Consolidate rental**. _Consolidate rental_ appears.
2. Its introductory _Consolidate rental_ page appears. Click **Next**.
3. **Select dongle** appears. **Next** is disabled until a listed dongle is selected. Select the dongle receiving your purchased rentals and click **Next**.
4. After a few seconds, **Dongle programmed successfully** indicates successful completion.

Check the programmed rental expiration dates with [Check licenses](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/programas-relacionados-con-licencias-y-llaves-de-proteccion/comprobar-licencias.md).
