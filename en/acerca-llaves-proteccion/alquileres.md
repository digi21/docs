# Rentals
<!-- id: alquileres -->
<!-- origen: aee8326d28b0c28094a31c5b55e5424765ee6916 -->

If you already have a hardware or software dongle, you can rent a license for our programs. Have your dongle's _Key ID_ ready. Find its _KeyID_ at [Sentinel Keys](http://localhost:1947/\_int\_/devices.html).

This table shows which versions you can use when renting:

| License type | Use end date | Program version | Purchase requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rental | Until the rental end date | Always the latest version. | None |

To rent:

1. Purchase a rental from our [rental store](http://www.digi21.net/Tienda/Alquiler).
2. Wait for an email titled **Rental confirmation**, stating that everything is ready. If it does not arrive immediately, do not call or email us; it may take a while.
3. Run [Consolidate rental](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/programas-relacionados-con-licencias-y-llaves-de-proteccion/consolidar-alquiler.md) to store the licenses in your dongle.

## Rental expiration warning

During the 30 days before your rental expires, each program startup displays a window giving the rental end date and time (UTC).
