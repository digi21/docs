# Computer with incorrect date
<!-- id: ordenador-fecha-incorrecta -->
<!-- origen: bb85185fbe7c407b0cb930e1b74618644842ab80 -->

The computer date is incorrect compared with the information stored in your dongle.

Three possible causes:

1. The operating system was reinstalled and the date was not set correctly.
2. The dongle was moved to a computer with an incorrect date.
3. The computer date was moved backward.

Our programs will not run with an incorrect date, to prevent illegal extensions of activation or rental expiration periods.

Correct the computer date and time before using any of our programs.
