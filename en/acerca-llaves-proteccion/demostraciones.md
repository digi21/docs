To try a program, request it through [Request a trial](https://www.digi21.net/SolicitarDemostracion).

You need an account on [https://www.digi21.net](https://www.digi21.net/) and a Digi21 dongle. Once licenses are ready, we email instructions to consolidate the rental in your dongle and begin using the program.
