If you have decided to purchase a hardware dongle:

1. Purchase through this [link](https://www.digi21.net/Tienda/Compra) \(select _Hasp HL Pro dongle_\).
2. Email [info@digi21.net](mailto:info@digi21.net) with:

* Company name.
* Tax identification number (N.I.F.).
* Dongle delivery address.
* Postal code.
* Contact person.
* Telephone.
* The email address you use to sign in to [www.digi21.net](https://www.digi21.net).

We will send the dongle to that address as soon as possible and add it to [My dongles](https://www.digi21.net/MisLlaves) for the [www.digi21.net](https://www.digi21.net/) user you specified.
