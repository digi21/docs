# Update your Hasp HL dongle firmware
<!-- id: actualizando-firmware -->
<!-- origen: 212b64dd98704ec9c86a5a32067103ae1a1c7f24 -->

If your dongle's _firmware_ is older than 3.25, update it to that version with [_Hasp HL Firmware Update_](https://digi21.blob.core.windows.net/download/FirmwareUpdate.exe). This quick, safe process updates firmware **without affecting** information already stored, so no previous-version program licenses are lost.

Follow these steps:

1. Download, extract and run [_Hasp HL Firmware Update_](https://digi21.blob.core.windows.net/download/FirmwareUpdate.exe).
2. A _Sentinel HASP Rus_ window appears.
3. Click _Apply Update._\
   Updating begins. **Do not disconnect the dongle from the computer** during the update.\
   This may take several minutes.
4. Completion is reported with _Update written successfully._
