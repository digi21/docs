### Obtain a dongle

Obtaining a dongle is straightforward:

* If you have a green Hasp HL dongle with one of our programs purchased before October 1, 2014, such as Digi3D 2007, we need to modify it before you can request trials, rent or upgrade to our modern programs. Follow [Update your Hasp HL dongle](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/obtener-una-llave-de-proteccion/actualizando-tu-llave-de-proteccion/actualizando-llave-hasp-hl/).
* If you have no dongle, [create](obtener-una-llave-de-proteccion/creando-llave-provisional.md) a temporary software dongle yourself. It lasts up to three months, after which you must purchase a dongle from our online [Store](https://www.digi21.net/Tienda/Compra).
* You can also purchase a permanent dongle from our online [Store](https://www.digi21.net/Tienda/Compra).

    * Select **Hasp HL Pro dongle** for hardware. We recommend this if you are in Spain, do not plan to send it abroad and can wait one or two days for courier delivery.
    * Select **Hasp SL (software)** for a software dongle. We recommend this if you are outside Spain, want immediate access to our programs or plan to email it to someone else.

    Learn more about hardware versus software in [Dongle types](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/tipos-de-llaves-proteccion.md).
