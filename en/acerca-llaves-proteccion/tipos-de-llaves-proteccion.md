# Dongle types
<!-- id: tipos-de-llaves-proteccion -->
<!-- origen: e1ddf7ffe7b1eab76b83d3a82445239ec5dd5319 -->

There are two dongle types: _hardware_ and _software_. Each has advantages over the other.

A _hardware_ dongle gives a sense of ownership because it is tangible. A _software_ dongle does not, but it is faster and can be emailed immediately to a collaborator abroad, a major advantage. It also occupies no _USB_ port and cannot be stolen unless the whole computer is stolen; a _hardware_ dongle is more vulnerable to theft.

This table summarizes their advantages and disadvantages.

| Advantage | Hardware | Software |
|---|---|---|
| Sense of ownership | Yes | No |
| Availability | Slow. After purchase, _Safenet_ sends it to us, we program it and send it to you. This may take a week. | Immediate. Create a temporary software dongle yourself by following [Create a temporary software dongle](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/obtener-una-llave-de-proteccion/creando-llave-provisional.md). |
| Speed | Slow | Fast |
| Mobility | Unplug it and connect it to another computer. | Move it between computers with [ActualizarLlaveLDK](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/obtener-una-llave-de-proteccion/actualizando-tu-llave-de-proteccion/actualizando-llave-hasp-hl/). |
| Mobility restrictions | None | Activation on another computer is allowed only after activation expires on the previously used computer. |
| Theft protection | Yes, provided activation is for no longer than one month. We recommend monthly activation. If our servers confirm the stolen dongle is not activated for longer than one month, we block it permanently from reactivation and add the license to the new dongle you purchase. | |
| Our recommendation | Companies without geographic mobility. | Companies needing geographic mobility to send licenses to remote employees or collaborators. |
