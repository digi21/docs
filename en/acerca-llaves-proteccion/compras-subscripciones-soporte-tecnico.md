# Purchases and technical support subscriptions

If you already have a hardware or software dongle, you can purchase a license for any of our programs. Otherwise create a temporary dongle by following [Create a temporary software dongle](/acerca-llaves-proteccion/obtener-una-llave-de-proteccion/creando-llave-provisional.md).

This table shows usable versions depending on whether you bought a new program or a year of technical support and updates, and the requirements for each purchase.

| License type | Use end date | Program version | Purchase requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase | Unlimited | Versions published up to **two years** after purchase (technical support expiration). Later versions cannot be used, so retain a version published before your support expiration. | None |
| Purchase of one year of technical support and updates. | Unlimited | Versions published up to **one year** after purchase (technical support expiration). Later versions cannot be used, so retain a version published before your support expiration. | Own a purchased product, regardless of version, and be its original owner. A license purchased from a third party does not permit this purchase; you can only rent. |

## Technical support expiration warning

One month before technical support expires, each program startup displays a warning dialog. We also email a reminder advising you to download and safely retain a backup of the program installer, because **we do not retain** an installer archive.
