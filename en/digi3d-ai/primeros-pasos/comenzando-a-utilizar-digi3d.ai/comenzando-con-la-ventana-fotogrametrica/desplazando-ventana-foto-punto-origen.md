# Moving the view to the model's origin
<!-- id: desplazando-ventana-foto-punto-origen -->
<!-- origen: d4438b4b5b172a413d12fc0599279028859cc8be -->

Sometimes you [snap](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-de-dibujo/primer-tentativo.md) to an entity outside your stereo model's bounds, or make a large movement with your input device, and the photogrammetric window moves to coordinates where there is no image.

If you have no control point file and do not know the coordinates of a point inside the model, you can move the photogrammetric window to the model's origin coordinates.

* Open **Photogrammetric window** and select **Go to point of origin**.

Check that the photogrammetric window has moved to the model's origin.
