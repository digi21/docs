# Orienting frame camera photogrammetric models
<!-- id: orientacion-de-modelos-fotogrametricos -->
<!-- origen: 6fcdb8320ad4208c3aaad06299b0d2b1d0504158 -->

This tutorial explains how to perform the orientations required to digitize on a frame camera photogrammetric model.

If the project has been aerial triangulated and you know the orientations of all its images, you do not need to perform internal, relative and absolute orientation manually. If you have no aerial triangulation calculation, perform the orientations manually, model by model.

To follow this tutorial, locate the directory where you unzipped the **Bronchales** project and delete all its files and subdirectories except these files:

* 107.TIF
* 108.TIF
* 109.TIF
* Bronchales.prj
* Bronchales.xyz
* RMK15.cam

Frame camera models require three orientations before they are ready for work:

1. [Internal orientation](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-fotogrametrica/sensor-camara-conica/orientacion-de-modelos-fotogrametricos/orientacion-interna/).
2. [Relative orientation](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-fotogrametrica/sensor-camara-conica/orientacion-de-modelos-fotogrametricos/orientacion-relativa/).
3. [Absolute orientation](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-fotogrametrica/sensor-camara-conica/orientacion-de-modelos-fotogrametricos/orientacion-absoluta/).
