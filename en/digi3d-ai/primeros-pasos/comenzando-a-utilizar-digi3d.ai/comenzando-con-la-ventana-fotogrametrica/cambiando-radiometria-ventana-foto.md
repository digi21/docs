# Changing photogrammetric image radiometry
<!-- id: cambiando-radiometria-ventana-foto -->
<!-- origen: 07f584caa89dbd3a0b394a3abf44c89db9ee5f42 -->

Change brightness, contrast and gamma at any time.

1. Open **Photogrammetric window** and select **Go to control point...**.
2. In **Select the control point where to move the photogrammetric window**, select **ZonaOrcura**.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Check that the window moves to a warehouse's shadow. A tree there may be invisible at current brightness and contrast settings.
5. Open **Photogrammetric window** and select **Image properties...**.
6. Check that **Display properties** appears.
7. Move **Gamma** right to improve dark-area visibility and reveal the tree.
8. Try changing brightness and contrast.
9. Click **Default** to restore _Brightness, Contrast_ and _Gamma_.
10. Close **Display properties**.
