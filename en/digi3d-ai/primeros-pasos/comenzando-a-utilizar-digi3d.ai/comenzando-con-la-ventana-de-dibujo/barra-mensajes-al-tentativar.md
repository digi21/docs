# Snap message bar and Snaps pane
<!-- id: barra-mensajes-al-tentativar -->
<!-- origen: 5f13370d65389268149c70c1e00eb824cf024ee2 -->

Explore the snap message bar and pane:

1. **Open** **cartografía\_en\_etrs89.bind**.
2. **Snap** to any entity with the mouse [snap button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers). Digi3D.AI's **message bar**, at the lower left, describes the snapped entity. Select different entities and observe their information.
3. Open **Snaps** through **Window/Snaps** to see selected entity history, in reverse selection order.
   * All codes of multicoded entities appear in **Snaps**.
