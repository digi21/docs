# Inserting vertices at known coordinates
<!-- id: insertando-vertices-coordenadas -->
<!-- origen: ceb99dc7a927a53382ad45d3b473676bd90ec2f8 -->

Follow these steps to insert vertices at known coordinates:

1. Click [Enter coordinates](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/x/xy.md) on the [Coordinates toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/coordenadas.md).
2. In **Manual insertion of coordinates**, enter the vertex coordinates, for example 100, 100; 200, 200; 100, 200; 100, 100. This sequence of coordinates draws a triangle.
   * If you are drawing a **LINE** and enter a series of vertices with [**XY**](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/x/xy.md), the line changes to pass through those vertices.
   * If the active code is linear, Digi3D.AI generates a line with the vertices you entered.

Click [here](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/x/xy.md) to see all the options available with XY.
