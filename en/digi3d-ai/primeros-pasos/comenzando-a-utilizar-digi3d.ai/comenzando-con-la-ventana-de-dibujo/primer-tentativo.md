# Your first snap
<!-- id: primer-tentativo -->
<!-- origen: c0543e21e47bbc1638bbff2eaf9cb68b07079d6d -->

Get to know Digi3D.AI's **snaps**, or **search modes**:

1. **Draw** a [line](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-de-dibujo\dibujando-primeras-lineas.md) with three vertices.
2. **Draw** a new line starting at the middle vertex of the first line.
3. **Zoom in** as far as possible on that vertex by scrolling the mouse wheel up. However far you zoom in, the two vertices will never coincide when you draw the second line. **Check the gap between the vertices** by zooming in on the area where they should coincide.
4. Click **Undo** on the [Undo toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/deshacer.md) to delete the last line drawn.
5. **Zoom out** by scrolling the mouse wheel down.

Draw the second line again using **snapping** (searching for points on an entity). Follow these steps:

1. Click the **middle mouse button** in an area without any entities. Digi3D.AI sounds an error to indicate that it found no entity in that area.
2. Select the entity you drew at the start with the **middle mouse button**. The entity is highlighted, and an animation shows the direction in which it was drawn.
3. **Cancel** the selection with the [cancel button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers) or **Esc**.
4. Find the [Snap toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/tentativo.md) and check that search mode **0. Vertex or projection in XY** is active. If it is not selected, click the arrow on the right and select it. If you cannot find the **Snap toolbar**, click [here](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-de-dibujo/barras-herramientas-basicas.md) to display it.
5. Try snapping to different positions on the entity by clicking the line with the mouse's [snap button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers). In search mode **0. Vertex or projection in XY**, Digi3D.AI first tries to find a vertex and, if none is found, tries to find a projection. Click the [cancel button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers) to cancel the snap.
6. Change the cursor size on the **Snap toolbar**, either by clicking **Change cursor size** repeatedly or by clicking the arrow to the right of the button and selecting a size from the drop-down list.
7. Move the cursor close to the first entity's middle vertex so you can snap to it.
8. Click the **middle mouse button** and check that the entity's middle vertex is selected.
9. Click the mouse's [data button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers) to accept the snapped vertex and continue the line.
