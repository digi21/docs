# Moving entities
<!-- id: mover-entidades -->
<!-- origen: bdab70714226a4cb697de170fdd35f92e711383b -->

Follow these steps to **move entities**:

1. **Draw** two entities like those in the video by clicking the drawing window with the mouse's [data button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers): a polyline with 3 vertices and a triangle.
2. **Display** the [Move toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/mover.md). If it is not active, click [here](/digi3d-ai/primeros-pasos/comenzando-a-utilizar-digi3d.ai/comenzando-con-la-ventana-de-dibujo/barras-herramientas-basicas.md) to learn how to display it.
3. **Click** the first button on the [Move toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/mover.md). It runs [MOVE](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/m/mover.md). The aim is to make the triangle's top vertex coincide with the polyline's bottom vertex.
4. In the lower left corner, **Digi3D.AI** indicates that [MOVE](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/m/mover.md) is running.
5. **Click** the [MOVE](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/m/mover.md) button again.
6. On the [Snap toolbar](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/tentativo.md), select **4. Nearest vertex in XYZ** from the drop-down list.
7. **Select** the triangle's vertex with the mouse's [snap button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers).
8. **Click** the **data button** to accept.
9. **Move** the entity to the polyline's vertex. Snap to the polyline's vertex to make the triangle's vertex coincide with it.
10. **Snap** to the polyline near the vertex with the mouse's [snap button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers). Digi3D.AI highlights the polyline and automatically moves the cursor to the vertex.
11. **Accept** with the mouse's [data button](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/programar-botones.md#mouse-button-numbers).
12. Finish [MOVE](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/m/mover.md) with the mouse.
