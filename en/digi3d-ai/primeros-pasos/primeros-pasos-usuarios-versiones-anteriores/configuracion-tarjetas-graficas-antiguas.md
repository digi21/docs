# Configuration for older graphics cards
<!-- id: configuracion-tarjetas-graficas-antiguas -->
<!-- origen: b839727c9f76a9e08a075a14729b6bcbaf0839f9 -->

Digi3D.AI requires a modern graphics card with at least 64 cores.

If your graphics card is old, such as an _NVidia Quadro FX 1400_, _NVidia Quadro FX 1500_ or _NVidia Quadro FX 3450/4000_, you will need to purchase a more modern one.
