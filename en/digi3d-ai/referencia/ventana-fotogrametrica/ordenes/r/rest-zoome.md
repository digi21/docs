# PLOT\_ZOOME
<!-- id: rest-zoome -->
<!-- origen: 510dd33923d4c38657ec7c9db01218820c284799 -->

Applies an extended zoom to the photogrammetric window's images.

## Parameters

None.

## Notes

Chooses the zoom factor that fits the entire left image in its view.

Cursor movement depends on [Extended zoom](/digi3d-ai/referencia/cuadros-de-dialogo/configuracion/ordenes-comunes-de-digi3d/zoom-extendido.md) in the configuration dialog:

* **Change the zoom factor and move the equipment to the center of the model.**, the default: moves the cursor to the model origin, then changes the zoom factor.
* **Change the zoom factor without moving the equipment to the center of the model.**: changes the zoom factor without moving the cursor.
