# GO\_TO\_ORIGIN\_POINT
<!-- id: ir-a-punto-origen -->
<!-- origen: 9d79e4eb7aa026bc207ce0122f4725446bb21c94 -->

GO_TO_ORIGIN_POINT moves the plotter to the center of the stereoscopic model.

## Parameters

None.

## Notes

Use **GO\_TO\_ORIGIN\_POINT** to move the plotter to the model origin when it is outside the model or you want to move it to that point.
