# LOWERZOOM
<!-- id: zoominf -->
<!-- origen: 2c0865bda6f8bfb20922236f07b2960d0bb31701 -->

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Set the overlap percentage with [OVERLAP](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/s/solape.md).
