# ZOOMW
<!-- id: zoomv -->
<!-- origen: 9b7419ca1321c1de31f3f148932aa341f84b20f6 -->

Display the part of the drawing enclosed by a window you define.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Specify two points on the screen to define the window.
