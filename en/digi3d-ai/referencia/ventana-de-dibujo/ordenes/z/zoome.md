# ZOOME
<!-- id: zoome -->
<!-- origen: b862e1b86eadd937b4b05dcea3b1db6eb4285e43 -->

Zoom to drawing extents.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Adjust the zoom factor to display the entire drawing on the screen. The camera also returns to the standard viewpoint orientation.
