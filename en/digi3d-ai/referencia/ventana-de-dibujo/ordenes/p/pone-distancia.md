# PUT\_DISTANCE
<!-- id: pone-distancia -->
<!-- origen: 51d15074f51d86a1ccc737d0034152d1581aec8b -->

Place text with the distance between two points.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Specify two points. Text contains their horizontal distance \(in XY, ignoring Z\) and is placed at their midpoint.

Use rotation, height, and justification from [AA](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/a/aa.md), [TH](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/a/at.md), and [JT](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/j/jt.md), respectively. Decimal places come from [NDEC](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/n/ndec.md). Store the text with the active code.
