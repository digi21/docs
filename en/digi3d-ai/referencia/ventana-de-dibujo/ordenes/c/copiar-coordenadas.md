# COPY\_COORDINATES
<!-- id: copiar-coordenadas -->
<!-- origen: e705da6ee9cc56f63dfd1bff51b99daa24cd34ab -->

Copy the coordinates of a digitized point to the clipboard.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Specify a point by digitizing with the data button, snapping to an entity with the snap button and accepting the point, or entering coordinates with [XY](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/x/xy.md).

Copy its X, Y, and Z coordinates, separated by spaces and using the drawing window's decimal places, in the same format as the [Coordinates](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/coordenadas.md) toolbar. Display the copied text in a balloon and end.

The reset button cancels without copying anything.

To copy currently displayed coordinates without digitizing a point, use **Copy coordinates to the clipboard** on the [Coordinates](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/coordenadas.md) or [Snap](/digi3d-ai/referencia/barras-de-herramientas/tentativo.md) toolbar.
