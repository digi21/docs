# ESCAPE
<!-- id: escape -->
<!-- origen: 450b569f0a346e4dc3ef33a37fdbdab295def613 -->

Finish the active command.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Pressing Esc, or any other key assigned to _ESCAPE_ in the corresponding keyboard menu, also runs this command.
