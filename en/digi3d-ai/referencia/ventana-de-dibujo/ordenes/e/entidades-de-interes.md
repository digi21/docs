# ENTITIES\_OF\_INTEREST
<!-- id: entidades-de-interes -->
<!-- origen: 1910616436104d5839794b6881f71f2688ef182a -->

Specify entities of interest on which entity modification commands will act.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Select entities with a multiple selection command, such as [SELECT\_COD](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/s/selecciona-cod.md). Mark selected entities as entities of interest and clear that mark from all other entities in all drawing files.
