# NEW\_PROJECT
<!-- id: nuevo-proyecto -->
<!-- origen: a80e88671eb14e00adef5a9224dd4ad893404a55 -->

Display the dialog box to open a photogrammetric model or drawing file.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

You cannot open two drawing files simultaneously.
