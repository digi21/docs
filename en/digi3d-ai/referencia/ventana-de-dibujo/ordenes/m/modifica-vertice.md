# MODIFYVERTEX
<!-- id: modifica-vertice -->
<!-- origen: 295ba4996dd5e29df10db35e21c346f7e35b6032 -->

Change the position of a vertex in a line or polygon.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Selecting the entity attaches the selected vertex to the cursor, and the application prompts for its new position. Only entities in the current model can be selected.
