# MOVE\_CAMERA\_FPS
<!-- id: mover-camara-fps -->
<!-- origen: 1f6a7df4793c89891d05f5aaacd961eccf5103b0 -->

Move the perspective camera using first-person game controls.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

While the command is active, the cursor is hidden and captured in the drawing window:

* Horizontal mouse movement rotates the camera around the vertical axis \(kappa\), and vertical movement tilts it \(omega\).
* `W` and `S` move forward and backward along the viewing direction; `A` and `D` move the camera left and right.
* Press Esc to finish the command.
