# SEE\_ALL\_ENTITIES
<!-- id: ver-todas-entidades -->
<!-- origen: 8999ced665294acc7a17673464da1a0204c2a512 -->

Enable the display of all entities.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

The command shows entities hidden by commands such as [SEE\_ONLY\_SELECTED\_ENTITIES](ver-solo-entidades-seleccionadas.md) or [SEE\_ONLY\_WITH\_BBDD\_LINK](ver-solo-con-enlace-bbdd.md) again. It does not enable disabled codes.
