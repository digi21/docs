# INTERPOLATE\_Z\_BETWEEN\_VERTICES
<!-- id: interpola-z-entre-vertices -->
<!-- origen: 1ba716fb738eab8341592d62ec2cc13e6b1947aa -->

Select a line at one of its vertices, then select another vertex on the same line. Selecting the second vertex interpolates Z coordinates of intermediate vertices in proportion to the plan \(XY\) distance traveled along the line. Both selected vertices retain their Z coordinates.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

If quality control rejects the modified entity, the original is retained unchanged.
