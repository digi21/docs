# UNLOAD\_FLOOD\_TOPOLOGY
<!-- id: descargar-topologia-inundacion -->
<!-- origen: d5070cd0a9869de0c1f8a702f9b8c4bf1d71c63b -->

Unload the topology for flooding from memory.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

The command removes from memory the temporary topology generated by [GENERATE\_FLOOD\_TOPOLOGY](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/g/generar-topologia-inundacion.md) or [GENERATE\_FLOOD\_TOPOLOGY\_WITHOUT\_ISLANDS](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/g/generar-topologia-inundacion-sin-islas.md), clears enclosure highlighting, and refreshes the view. It does not modify the drawing file.

After unloading the topology for flooding, flooding commands display "No temporary topology is created" and end unless you regenerate the topology. **Flooding/Unload topology for flooding** is disabled while no topology exists.
