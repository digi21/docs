# DIGI3D
<!-- id: digi3d -->
<!-- origen: 49d9354bf82585b2217e6e639f06234a6bae2aed -->

Link the **DigiNG** cursor to **Digi3D** movements.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

The application automatically links the **DigiNG** cursor to **Digi3D** movements when you press a button or pedal assigned to **Data**, **Snap**, or **End entity**.
