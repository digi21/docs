# RECOVER
<!-- id: recupera -->
<!-- origen: 0b9c700054930a6aacf3ac20afaefc2d4dfb0509 -->

Restore elements marked as deleted in the drawing file. [DELETED](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/variables/b/borrados.md) must be enabled to restore these elements.

## Parameters

Does not accept parameters.

## Notes

Entities that are not deleted cannot be restored.
