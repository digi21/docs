# Immediate
<!-- id: inmediato -->
<!-- origen: 8621bdc11fd8e68fcf2a38fb95449be5c617500d -->

The Immediate menu provides operations while digitizing an entity.

**Copy coordinates to the clipboard**, after **Add...**, runs [COPY\_COORDINATES](/digi3d-ai/referencia/ventana-de-dibujo/ordenes/c/copiar-coordenadas.md) to copy the coordinates of a digitized point to the clipboard.
