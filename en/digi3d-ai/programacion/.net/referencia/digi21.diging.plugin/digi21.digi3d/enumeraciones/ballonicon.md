| Value | Number | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `None` | 0 | Balloon without an icon. |
| `Information` | 1 | Balloon with Digi3D.AI's information icon, a question mark. |
| `Warning` | 2 | Balloon with the warning icon. |
