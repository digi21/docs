## Operating system

Digi3D.AI is available only as a 64-bit version and is compatible with Windows 11.

## Graphics card

There are three types of graphics cards on the market:

1. Entry-level.
2. Mid-range.
3. Professional.

_Digi3D.AI_ requires a mid-range or professional graphics card compatible with _OpenGL_.

One difference between professional and mid-range cards \(apart from processing speed and memory capacity\) is that professional cards usually include an onboard _stereo connector_.

The stereo connector synchronizes Digi3D.AI's stereoscopic display with the glasses. With a graphics card that includes this connector \(and a synchronization cable connecting the card to the glasses' emitter\), viewing does not alternate between stereo and pseudostereo.

Some Digi3D.AI features require a graphics card with at least 32 cores.

* If you will use Digi3D.AI only to edit drawing files \(without loading stereo models\), you can use mid-range or high-end graphics cards.
* If you will load stereo models, your choice of graphics card depends on the 3D monitor:
  1. With an active polarization 3D monitor, such as VOXEL SPACE's, you can use mid-range or high-end graphics cards.
  2. If your stereo monitor is a _Stereo Mirror_, the graphics card does not need an onboard _stereo connector_. A professional card is therefore unnecessary; you can use mid-range or high-end cards.
  3. With an active stereo monitor, we recommend a professional graphics card with an onboard stereo connector.
  4. With a stereoscope, you can use mid-range or high-end graphics cards.
  5. With anaglyph viewing, you can use mid-range or high-end graphics cards.

## Stereo monitor

We recommend VOXEL SPACE's active polarization 3D monitor, available from the manufacturer's website: [http://www.voxelspace.com.cn/productd?product_id=922](http://www.voxelspace.com.cn/productd?product_id=922).

The monitor includes a filter that rapidly alternates circular polarization between clockwise and counterclockwise. It uses passive polarized glasses like those in 3D cinemas: they are lightweight and, unlike active glasses, have no shutters interrupting your view.

The blog post [Active polarization 3D monitor](https://www.digi21.net/Blog/monitor-3d-con-polarizacion-activa) explains its operation in more detail.

## Input device

Digi3D.AI supports multiple input devices.

* You can purchase handwheels and an encoder card by contacting us at [https://www.digi21.net/contactar](https://www.digi21.net/contactar).
* You can purchase any Stealth 3D Mouse device \(we recommend the S3Z\) from [http://stealth3dmouse.com](http://stealth3dmouse.com/).
* You can purchase a _Softmouse3D_ from _Global Geo Supplies_: [http://www.geosupplies.com](http://www.geosupplies.com/).
* For a more basic setup, you can work with several ordinary mice. We recommend a _Logitech Marble Mouse_: [http://www.logitech.com/es-es/product/4786?crid=8](http://www.logitech.com/es-es/product/4786?crid=8).
