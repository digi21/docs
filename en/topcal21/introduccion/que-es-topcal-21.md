# What is TopCal 21?
<!-- id: que-es-topcal-21 -->
<!-- origen: 0989629d16c2835f7a1b857228989ffd305a3607 -->

TopCal 21 is a tool for solving surveying and geodetic calculations and problems, such as the ones listed below.

TopCal 21 runs under 32-bit Windows operating systems. It works with projects, so you can organize your jobs in subdirectories and handle more than one point file and observation file at the same time. It also has a data display screen, or [user interface](interfaz-de-usuario.md), that works like a spreadsheet. In addition, all its tools are presented with guided dialog boxes that help the user with the calculation.

The program imports and exports data in total station file formats and in drawing file formats.

The output files with the results and calculations can have the format of blank-delimited ASCII, DXF, DIGI ASCII and BIN, DGN or HTML.

TopCal 21 has its own tools for solving surveying problems, such as:

* Computation of triangulations made up of [Intersections](../herramientas/intersecciones-directas/README.md) or [3-point resections](../herramientas/intersecciones-inversas/README.md)
* [Altimetric compensations](../herramientas/compensacion-de-altimetria/README.md)
* [Orientations](../herramientas/calculo-de-desorientaciones/README.md)
* [Traverses](../herramientas/poligonales/README.md)
* [Radial surveys](../herramientas/radiacion/README.md)
* [Surface computation](../calculos/calculo-de-superficies/README.md)
* Generation of drawings from the points
* [Transformation of coordinates between different systems](../herramientas/transformacion/README.md)
* Generation of [vertex data sheets](../calculos/generar-resenas-de-vertices/README.md) or [control point data sheets](../calculos/generar-resenas-de-puntos-de-apoyo/README.md)
* Computation of longitudinal profiles and cross sections
* Volume computation
* Computation of cuts and fills
* [Compensation of topographic networks by least squares](../herramientas/compensacion-por-minimos-cuadrados/README.md)
* Automatic reconstruction of the sketch from the encoding taken in the field
* Various geometric calculations, such as intersections between elements (straight lines, arcs…), conversion from polar to rectangular coordinates and vice versa, etc.
* Operations with observations, such as [computation of averages](../calculos/calculo-de-medias-de-visuales/README.md) or of [systematic errors](../calculos/calculo-medias-cd-ci/README.md).
