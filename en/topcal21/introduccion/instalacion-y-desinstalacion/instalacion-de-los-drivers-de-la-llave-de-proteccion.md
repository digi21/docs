# Installing the dongle drivers
<!-- id: instalacion-de-los-drivers-de-la-llave-de-proteccion -->
<!-- origen: 81ff1737bae793c564b60d76f6fc71d47fa3e35d -->

If you will use a dongle and its drivers are not installed on the computer, install the dongle drivers:

* Select the dongle drivers in the list of programs available on the CD.
* Follow the instructions shown by the installer program.
* Connect the dongle to the corresponding USB port of the computer.

> [!NOTE]
> Topcal21 can also be protected with a software license instead of a dongle. If the computer has a software license activation, you do not need to install the drivers or connect the dongle. See [Software licenses](../../../licencias-de-software/README.md).
