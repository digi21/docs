# Uninstallation
<!-- id: topcal21-desinstalacion -->
<!-- origen: f3171bca795377c3e2f192dde2b880c76546fa9d -->

To uninstall TopCal 21, use the *Add or Remove Programs* tool available in the Windows *Control Panel*, as follows:

* Select *Start* → *Control Panel*.
* Double-click *Add or Remove Programs*.
* Select TopCal 21 from the list of programs that can be removed.
* Click the **Add or Remove** button.
* Follow the instructions of the installer.
