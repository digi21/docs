# Create an account and send your first technical support ticket
<!-- id: creando-cuenta-soporte-tecnico -->
<!-- origen: 3cddcbb57a8233add55786f9f8a7274404ce8376 -->

Digi21 technical support works within [www.digi21.net](https://www.digi21.net). Creating tickets requires an account there. If you already have one, for example from downloading Digi21 software, use it.

## Create an account

1. Open [https://www.digi21.net](https://www.digi21.net) and click **Register** at the top right.
2. Complete **Create an account**:
   * In **Email address**, enter your sign-in email. Access codes and ticket notifications are sent there.
   * In **Name** and **Last name**, enter your personal name, not your organization's, so support knows whom it is addressing.
   * For a company account, enter the company name in **Company** and its C.I.F. in **N.I.F.** Otherwise leave **Company** empty and enter your N.I.F.
   * Complete **Phone** and the postal address.
3. Click **Register**.

The account is created and signed in. It has no password.

## Sign in

Each sign-in is confirmed with a 6-digit emailed code.

1. Open [https://www.digi21.net/Soporte](https://www.digi21.net/Soporte). If signed out, **Sign in** appears. You can also click **Sign in** at the top right.
2. Enter the account email.
3. Check **Keep me signed in on this computer** to avoid signing in each time you close the browser. The session lasts 14 days from the last visit.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. Copy the emailed code into **Code** and click **Verify**. It expires after 5 minutes.

If it does not arrive, check spam and add `servidor@digi21.net` to contacts. Click **Resend code** to request another.

## Create a ticket

1. Open [https://www.digi21.net/Soporte](https://www.digi21.net/Soporte), or choose **My technical support tickets** from **Technical support** on the website.
2. Click **Create ticket**.
3. Select the relevant application in **Application**.
4. Summarize the issue in one sentence in **Subject**.
5. In **Problem description**, explain what you tried, what you expected and what happened. Include error messages, reproduction steps and program version.
6. Drag relevant files into **Attachments**, or click to select them. See [Attach files to a technical support ticket](/soporte-tecnico/adjuntando-archivos-grandes-soporte-tecnico.md).
7. Click **Create ticket**.

Read the [technical support ticket rules](/soporte-tecnico/reglas-soporte-tecnico.md) before creating one.

The website opens the ticket and emails _We have received your ticket #number_. The relevant program's support team receives the ticket by email then.

## View your tickets

**My technical support tickets** ([https://www.digi21.net/Soporte](https://www.digi21.net/Soporte)) lists all your tickets with number, subject, application, status (**Open** or **Closed**) and last activity date.

Click a subject for the full conversation and attachments.

## Reply to a ticket

When support replies, you receive an email titled _[Ticket #number] Reply from technical support_ with its text and a ticket link.

To reply:

1. Open the ticket through the email link or **My technical support tickets**.
2. Write in the **Reply** box.
3. Drag files into that section's attachment area or click to select them.
4. Click **Send reply**.

A message can contain text, files or both.

## Share your desktop

If support needs to see your screen, it will ask in the ticket. Then click **Share my desktop** on **My technical support tickets**. This downloads the program support uses to connect to your computer.
