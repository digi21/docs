# Close a technical support ticket
<!-- id: cerrando-ticket-soporte-tecnico -->
<!-- origen: 9140a2cb557cb663915f19065a81bcafcec63de0 -->

A ticket has one of two states:

* **Open**: work is in progress.
* **Closed**: resolved or expired through lack of response.

**My technical support tickets** ([https://www.digi21.net/Soporte](https://www.digi21.net/Soporte)) shows each state in **Status**.

## Closure by technical support

The support team closes tickets. The website has no button for you to close one yourself.

When resolved, reply in the ticket to say so, for example: _The problem is resolved, thank you_. Support will close it.

## Automatic closure without a reply

If the last message is from support and you do not reply, the website closes the ticket automatically:

1. After 15 days without a reply, it emails _[Ticket #number] Awaiting your reply_ with the closure date.
2. After 30 days without a reply, it closes the ticket and emails _[Ticket #number] Closed due to inactivity_.

Both periods run from support's last message. Replying cancels automatic closure.

## Reopen a ticket

Write in the ticket's **Reply** section and click **Send reply**. It returns to **Open** and support receives your message.

For a new, unrelated question, create a new ticket. See [technical support ticket rules](/soporte-tecnico/reglas-soporte-tecnico.md).
